# Taste is vocabulary

**Taste is being able to say what you like and predict what others will like. Name the flaw in the field's own terms instead of asking for "better". Own what you ship by using it.**

## Why

- "Make it more beautiful" gives generic output. A named flaw gives a specific fix.
- Generic AI-built apps look alike because their makers couldn't say what was wrong.
- Generator and grader must keep pace. As models reach your level of taste, you need more words to keep improving the work.
- The real risk of AI is self-erasure: watching output pass by instead of caring about it.

## Evidence

- **Liu:** in art school his taste outran his skill. He could dislike something without knowing how to fix it. Learning words such as space, value, shape, texture and negative space closed the gap.
- **Liu:** a tweet asking people to "find all the implicit state machines in my app" sent developers to look up the term. Many then reported apps about 20% faster. A new word became a concrete improvement.
- **Liu:** asking an AI for a "more beautiful" 3D print kept giving him "rectangles glued together". He studied sports cars, industrial design and shop displays to learn what good curves are, and his designs improved. He studied typography, motion and interaction for the same reason.
- **Liu:** "to have good taste, you have to eat". Play games, use other people's tools, look at beautiful things, and watch films and ads to learn story.
- **Liu:** the author is whoever holds the vision, as a director owns a film made by thousands. Use your own product, notice what breaks, and iterate. The good examples online were iterated, not made in one shot.
- **Pocock:** QA is where taste enters, so keep it human. Agents judge visual design poorly; build throwaway prototypes and pick one.

## How we apply it

- at-game-design 1.4.0, Phase D "Name the flaw": name the flaw in the craft's terms, gather references, make one named change per iteration.
