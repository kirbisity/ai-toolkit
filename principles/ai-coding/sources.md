## How the talks relate

- They agree on most points. Agents are now strong; the limits are context, checks, safety and human judgement.
- Horthy and Pocock both cover context limits. Pocock credits the "smart zone" idea to Horthy.
- Tan, Hallie and Pocock all cover verification, from three angles: the codebase, the harness and tests.
- Liu is the only one on taste and ownership. His talk has the least tool detail.
